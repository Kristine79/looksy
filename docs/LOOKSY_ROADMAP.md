## 7. Risk Assessment

### 7.1 Technical Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| OpenAI API quality insufficient | Medium | High | Fallback to manual entry, test alternatives |
| pgvector performance at scale | Low | Medium | Monitor, migrate to Pinecone if needed |
| Supabase limits reached | Low | Medium | Upgrade plan, optimize queries |
| Vercel cold starts | Medium | Low | Edge functions, keep-alive |
| AI cost overrun | Medium | High | Rate limiting, caching, cheaper models |

### 7.2 Product Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Users don't trust AI | Medium | High | Trust Layer, transparency, control |
| Low engagement | Medium | High | Daily ritual, feedback loops |
| Fashion Memory feels creepy | Low | High | User control, transparency, opt-in |
| Pro conversion too low | Medium | Medium | Value demonstration, pricing tests |
| Competition from big players | Medium | Medium | Focus on niche, build moat |

### 7.3 Business Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Slow user acquisition | Medium | High | Marketing, referrals, partnerships |
| Low retention | Medium | High | Engagement features, value delivery |
| Funding gap | Low | High | Bootstrap, revenue-first approach |
| Team burnout | Medium | High | Sustainable pace, hiring plan |
| Regulatory changes | Low | Medium | Privacy-first, data portability |

### 7.4 Risk Response Plan

| Risk Level | Response |
|------------|----------|
| **Critical** | Immediate escalation, all-hands response |
| **High** | Weekly review, mitigation plan active |
| **Medium** | Monthly review, monitoring in place |
| **Low** | Quarterly review, accept or monitor |
